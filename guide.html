How to get the .zip?

  1. Log in to Spotify in your browser (not the app)

  2. Head over to spotify.com/us-en/account/privacy

  3. Scroll to the bottom of the page and find “Download your data”

  4. Tick the Select Extended streaming history box under Extended streaming history

  5. Click the “Request data” button

  6. In your email provider (Gmail, ProtonMail etc.), confirm the request of your data. You should receive a mail

  7. After a couple of days, click on the Download your data button in the new mail

Import the .zip on the main site and see your stats!
